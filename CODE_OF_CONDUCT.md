# Code of Conduct

HyperION is made for musicians, producers and creators of every background and skill level. This space should feel welcoming to all of them.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to music production.
- Give feedback about the software, not about the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and assume good intent.
- Credit other people's work and respect their rights.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind
- Sexual, violent or hateful content
- Sharing other people's private information
- Spam, advertising or repeated off-topic posts
- Posting cracked software, licence keys or copyrighted material you do not own

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and may block participants who break these rules. To report a problem privately, use the [contact page](https://www.hyperiondaw.com/contact) and mention "Code of Conduct" in your message.

This policy applies to this repository's issues, discussions and pull requests, and to official HyperION community spaces.
